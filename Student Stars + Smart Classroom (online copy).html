Change a logo, stamp or signature image (optional, already built in)

The Ministry logo, Zayed logo, stamp and signature are already inside this file, so nothing needs uploading and it works offline.

📊 Exam results, analysis and sending (optional)

Upload one or more Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), PDF or CSV files, or paste the table. Add several files (for example Exam 1, Exam 2, Exam 3) to compare them: students are matched by name. Columns: Name, Mark, Email (optional), Phone (optional, with country code), Group (optional, for team certificates). You can add an “Out of” column. PDF reading works for PDFs that contain real text, not scanned photos. Everything works offline. Only the WhatsApp, Telegram and email buttons need internet when you press them, and you attach the certificate PDF yourself.
